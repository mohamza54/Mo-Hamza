What I believe.

Four ideas I come back to on almost every product.

Name the tension first

Most products serve two groups who want different things. I write that conflict down before anyone drafts a requirement, because the best solutions usually come from it.

Find the number behind the behaviour

Opinions are cheap. A good metric tells you what people did, and a few conversations tell you why. I want both before committing a team.

Ship something small and real

A working prototype teaches more in a day than a spec does in a month. I prefer to be wrong early and cheaply.

Retention is the real product

Getting someone in the door is marketing. Giving them a reason to return is product. I design for the second visit first.

The problem

What I did
